# Atliq Hotels - Tableau Dashboard

## Overview
Built an interactive Tableau dashboard for Atliq Hotels to analyze booking performance and identify why revenue is leaking despite high bookings.

## Key Insights
- Mumbai and Bangalore generate highest revenue, but occupancy is low in some properties
- High cancellation rate in Luxury hotels is reducing overall revenue
- Direct bookings give more revenue than travel agents
- Weekends have higher bookings compared to weekdays

## Dashboard Highlights
- Revenue by City and Hotel
- Occupancy % and Cancellation Rate
- Booking Trend - May to July 2022
- Platform-wise Performance

## Dataset
- Period: May to July 2022
- Total Bookings: 1,34,590
- Hotels: 25 properties across India

## Tools Used
- Tableau for Dashboard
- Excel for Data Cleaning
